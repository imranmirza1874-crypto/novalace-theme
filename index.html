The Architecture & Anatomy +

The Novalace Sphère redefines feline play with architectural harmony. Unlike traditional scratching posts that shed cardboard debris, our sphere utilizes continuous hand-wound unbleached African sisal rope around a durable core, suspended on an arched solid oak chassis with sealed brass bearings for effortless, kinetic rolling motion.

Dimensions & Materials +
  • Frame: 100% Solid European Oak or Smoked Walnut (FSC-Certified)
  • Sphere: Natural unbleached organic sisal twine (0% chemical bleaching)
  • Hardware: Solid brushed champagne gold brass axle & quiet bearings
  • Grand Size: 34cm (H) x 30cm (W) x 22cm (D) • Sphere Ø 24cm • Weight 3.4kg
  • Petite Size: 26cm (H) x 24cm (W) x 18cm (D) • Sphere Ø 18cm • Weight 2.1kg
Care, Maintenance & Refill Spheres +

Wipe the solid wood frame with a dry or slightly damp linen cloth. The natural sisal core is designed for 3+ years of intense daily scratching. Replacement sisal spheres can be swapped in under 60 seconds with our included magnetic brass tool.

White-Glove Shipping & Guarantees +

Every piece is individually inspected and numbered at our atelier. Orders ship within 24 hours with express tracking. We provide a 10-year structural warranty on all solid wood frames.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I introduce the Novalace Sphère to a timid cat? +

Every Novalace Sphère includes a small vial of organic catnip botanical spray. Lightly mist the sisal sphere and gently spin the ball with your hand to demonstrate the kinetic movement. Most felines engage within 24 to 48 hours.

Does it wobble or slide across hardwood floors? +

Not at all. The solid wood chassis features a low center of gravity (3.4kg on Grand Classique) and is fitted with high-density natural Portuguese cork non-skid pads that protect delicate flooring while preventing movement.

How do replacement sisal balls work? +

Our core is 100% modular. When you wish to renew the sisal sphere after years of play, you can order a replacement sphere on our site and swap it in under 60 seconds with the provided brass hex wrench.

What is your 30-Day Feline Trial policy? +

We want both you and your cat to be completely in love. If within 30 days your companion hasn't embraced the Sphère, notify our concierge team for a prepaid return label and a 100% full refund.
