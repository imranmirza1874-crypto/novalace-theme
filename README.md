# Novalace — Modern Warm Luxury Pet E-Commerce Experience

> **Haute Feline Living &bull; Atelier**  
> Editorial E-Commerce UI for high-end architectural cat furniture and kinetic play sculptures. Handcrafted from FSC-certified solid European oak, dark smoked walnut, unbleached African sisal, and organic ceramics.

---

## 🌟 Pages & Features

### 1. `index.html` — Flagship Product Page (*The Novalace Sphère 2-in-1*)
* **Editorial Split Hero Grid**: High-resolution zoomable gallery with interactive 360° spin modal.
* **Dynamic Variant & Price Selector**: Finish selector (Natural FSC Oak, Smoked Walnut, Nordic White Ash) and sizing selector (Petite Ø18cm, Grand Classique Ø24cm).
* **Curated Add-on Upsells**: Organic Catnip Infusion Mist pairing.
* **Interactive Hotspot Anatomy**: Craftsmanship dive with animated hotspot pins linking to solid oak chassis, champagne brass bearings, and unbleached sisal.
* **Editorial Sanctuary Lookbook**: Side-by-side comparison table (Novalace Sphère vs. generic cardboard scratchers).
* **Verified Customer Photo Wall & Review Filters**: Star rating breakdown and verified owner quotes.
* **Slide-Over Shopify Cart Drawer**: Real-time free shipping threshold tracker with animated progress bar.
* **Multi-Currency Live Engine**: Instant conversion across USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), and JPY (¥).
* **Sticky Bottom Add-to-Bag Bar**: Glides into view when scrolling past hero conversion actions.

### 2. `collection.html` — Catalog Page (*Sculptural Living & Mindful Play*)
* **Top Editorial Banner & Toolbar**: Category kickers, active result counts, sort engine, and 3-column / 2-column view toggles.
* **Minimalist Left Sidebar Filters**: Categories, dynamic price range slider ($20–$400), FSC material swatches, and in-stock toggle.
* **Main 3-Column Luxury Product Grid**: 6 curated objects with secondary hover angle transition, quick-view inspector, and quick-add actions.
* **Mid-Page Manifesto Banner**: *"Designed to Protect Fine Furniture While Honoring Natural Instincts"*.
* **Feline Behavioral Science FAQ**: Deep accordion explanations on biomechanics, non-toxic glazes, and zero-shredding sisal weave.
* **Figma 9:16 Mobile Mockup Frame Mode**: One-click toggle for Behance & Dribbble presentation view.
