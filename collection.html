Feline Behavioral Science

The Science of Harmonious Living

How biomechanics and intentional design eliminate friction between pets and pristine interiors.

Why do spherical scratchers satisfy cats better than vertical flat posts? +

A spherical geometry engages both forepaws concurrently, simulating the kinetic resistance of natural fallen timber. As the ball rotates smoothly on precision brass bearings, cats experience dynamic visual feedback that activates their predatory neural pathways without becoming overstimulated.

How does Novalace eliminate the messy debris typical of cardboard scratchers? +

Standard cardboard scratchers rely on pressed paper pulp bound with industrial adhesives, which shear into messy flakes under claw impact. Novalace utilizes long-staple, unbleached African sisal fibers spun under high tension and bound with organic cornstarch. Claws penetrate between the fibers to shed dead husk sheaths without cutting the twine.

Are all wood finishes, glazes, and mists non-toxic and feline-safe? +

Yes, uncompromisingly. All solid oak and walnut timbers are hand-finished exclusively with food-safe organic beeswax and cold-pressed linseed oils (zero VOCs). Our ceramic fountains use heavy-metal-free, lead-free food grade glazes, and our botanical mists are pure steam-distilled organic hydrolats.

What is the 30-Day In-Home Feline Trial? +

We encourage you to place our objects in your living sanctuary for a full month. If your feline companion does not naturally bond with the sculpture within 30 days, our concierge team will arrange a complimentary return courier and issue a 100% full refund.
